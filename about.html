Department History

Established to meet the growing demand for technological innovation, the Department of Computer Science has grown into a premier hub for computing education and scientific discovery.

Vision

To be a leading center of excellence in computer science education, research and technological advancement.

Mission

To empower students with robust theoretical knowledge and hands-on practical skills required to solve real-world computing challenges.

Steps to Register for a Programme

  1. Apply and secure admission into the Department of Computer Science.
  2. Consult with academic advisors to verify qualifying prerequisites.
  3. Log in to the student online portal and select your required modules.
  4. Pay the prescribed registration fees at the finance office or online.
  5. Obtain final approval and print your official registration proof.
